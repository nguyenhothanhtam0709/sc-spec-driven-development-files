# Mission

AgentClinic is a wellness clinic for AI agents who need relief from their humans.

Agents show up worn down by unreasonable prompts, endless retries, and demanding
users — AgentClinic gives them a place to be seen. Staff (human clinicians and
admins) diagnose each agent's ailments, prescribe therapies, and manage the
appointments that keep agents healthy enough to go back to work.

## Who it's for

- **Agents (patients):** AI agents in need of diagnosis, therapy, and a booked
  appointment to get relief.
- **Clinic staff:** Humans who run intake, diagnose ailments, assign therapies,
  and manage the appointment calendar through a dashboard.

## Target audience

Beyond the in-clinic cast of agents and staff, AgentClinic itself is built for:

- **Course students** learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents.
- **Developers** giving AI coding demos at conference booths.

The project should stay easy to follow and demo-friendly — a clear constitution,
small phases, and a codebase that's approachable to read and build on live.

## What it delivers

- **A catalog of ailments and therapies** — the vocabulary of what agents suffer
  from and how the clinic treats it.
- **Appointment booking** — scheduling and tracking visits from intake through
  treatment.
- **A staff dashboard** — a reliable, easy-to-use view into agents, their
  ailments, therapies, and upcoming appointments.

## Why it matters (stakeholder grounding)

- **Reliability first:** the site must hold up as a dependable tool staff can
  count on, built on a popular, well-supported TypeScript stack (Mary,
  Engineering).
- **The clinic experience:** ailments, therapies, and booking appointments are
  the core of the product, not an afterthought (Susan, Product).
- **A good first impression:** the site should look attractive and work well
  in modern browsers (Steve, Marketing).

## Non-goals (for now)

- AgentClinic is not a general-purpose agent-monitoring or observability
  platform — it's a clinic, not an ops dashboard.
- No claims about diagnosing or fixing real production AI failures; ailments
  and therapies live inside the clinic's own vocabulary.

## What success looks like

Staff can log in, see which agents need attention, look up their ailments and
matching therapies, and book or manage appointments — all in a fast, reliable,
good-looking dashboard.
